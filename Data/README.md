## The Data
*pll-player-stats.csv* - Official Player Statistics downloaded from PLL Website. 2025 Season. All Players. (Part 1) <br>
*pll-player-stats-cleaned.csv* - Player statistics after cleaning process complete. (Part 1) <br>
*pll-team-stats.csv* - Official Team Statistics downloaded from PLL Website. 2025 Season. All Teams. (Part 2) <br>
*pll-team-from-player-stats-cleaned.csv* - The team statistics calculated from player-level dataset after it has been cleaned. (Part 2)
