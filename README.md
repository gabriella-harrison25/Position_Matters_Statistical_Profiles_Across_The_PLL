This is a project in progress. Pages will be updated as work is completed.


## Background

The Premier Lacrosse League (PLL) is a professional lacrosse league created in 2018 by Paul and Mike Rabil. It consists of eight teams split into two conferences, East and West. The regular season is made up of ten weekends with a break for the all-star weekend in the middle. The PLL is one of the fastest-growing sports leagues in the United States, and it is expected to continue growing in popularity. 

Each year, PLL holds a draft to allow each team the opportunity to change its roster. Each team tries to create a combination of players that maximizes their wins during the regular season in order to make it to the Post-Season Playoffs. 

## The Project
This exploratory data analysis (EDA) seeks to analyze individual players' and teams' performance during the 2025 season to predict future performance. An analysis based on player position, team composition, and season performance was used to conclude the strongest teams entering the 2026 season.
